How we estimated this

The green index runs from −1 (left) through 0 (up) to +1 (right). +0.35 is a synthetic placeholder; its scoring formula is not defined and it is independent of the period comparison. The percentage under Over time compares known emissions with the named reference. Unknown emissions are excluded, not zero. Treemap categories contain their underlying activities; areas use known estimates only, never filler. Small labels are hidden when they do not fit; focus a category or open an activity for detail. Map locations describe a fictional routine around Hang Hau, campus and the city; a trip marker attributes an activity. Interpolated journey points can be inspected in the activity workspace.

Coverage

    Household readings and personal activities are labelled on each record. Household energy is allocated once to this person; recorded appliance subtotals do not overlap. Missing or unresolved activities are excluded from carbon totals, never treated as zero.
