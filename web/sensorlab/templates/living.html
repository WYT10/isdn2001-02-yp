How we estimated this

Demo comparison index = (reference − selected) / reference, clipped to −1…+1. −1 left, 0 up, +1 right. This is not a validated green-living score. Unknown emissions are excluded, not zero.

Coverage

    Household readings and personal activities are labelled on each record. This combined view is not a per-person household allocation. Missing or unresolved activities are excluded from carbon totals, never treated as zero.

    Labels show activity totals in kg CO₂e. Contours and 3D height show the same smoothed density, with height exaggerated for clarity; they do not measure air pollution or reconstruct a travel route.

    How we estimated this

    CO₂e means carbon dioxide equivalent. Demo factors and the named reference period are disclosed on each record. No personal reduction target or uncertainty bounds have been validated. These inputs are activity estimates; raw sensor pulses are not automatically converted to carbon.

    Future connections: home water and socket energy measurements → versioned conversion factors; outside activities → user-confirmed app/GPS events; reflection prompts → explicit notification preferences. This preview does not track location or send background notifications.
