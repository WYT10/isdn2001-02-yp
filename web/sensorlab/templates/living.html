How we estimated this

The green index runs from −1 (left) through 0 (up) to +1 (right). +0.35 is a synthetic placeholder; its scoring formula is not defined and it is independent of the period comparison. The percentage under Over time compares known emissions with the named reference. Unknown emissions are excluded, not zero. Treemap categories contain their underlying activities; areas use known estimates only, never filler. Small labels are hidden when they do not fit; focus a category or open an activity for detail. Map locations are synthetic examples around Mong Kok, Yau Ma Tei and Jordan; a trip marker is an attribution point, not a traced route.
