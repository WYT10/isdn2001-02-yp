Data and calculation method

CO₂e means carbon dioxide equivalent. No emission factors, personal baseline, reduction target or uncertainty bounds have been supplied. These inputs are activity estimates; raw sensor pulses are not automatically converted to carbon.

Future connections: home water and socket energy measurements → versioned conversion factors; outside activities → user-confirmed app/GPS events; reflection prompts → explicit notification preferences. This preview does not track location or send background notifications.
